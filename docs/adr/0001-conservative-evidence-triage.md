# Evidence Coverage is Conservative Triage

Paper T-Rail serves researchers auditing their own drafts; its report is a traceable triage aid, not a certification or grade. A Citation Context is the smallest citation-bearing clause, with sentence fallback; separate clause contexts do not share targets, and claim-to-target links within each context are inferred and deduplicated by source span. Evidence strength considers role, directness, claim scope, study design, relevance, and calibrated judgement—not raw model confidence alone. Comparable support and contradiction yields `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`; clearly stronger support or contradiction may determine the result, while partial support without a stronger contradiction yields `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`. Abstract-only and unsupported-language papers are not semantically judged in V1. Human Reviews are append-only assessments kept separate from machine judgements and never rewrite machine results. We prefer visible uncertainty and provenance over silently guessing citation intent or overstating confidence.
